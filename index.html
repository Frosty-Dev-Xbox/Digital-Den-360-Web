Features

Everything in one store

Built for the homebrew community, by the homebrew community.

🎮

Games

Homebrew games built for the Xbox 360 — shooters, platformers, fighters, and more.

🕹️

Emulators

Retro consoles on your 360. NES, SNES, Genesis, N64, and more.

🛠️

Utilities

Dashboards, tools, patches, and everything you need to keep your 360 running smooth.

🎨

Themes

Custom dashboard skins to give your console a fresh look.

📦

One-Click Install

Downloads install straight to your console. No PC, no USB, no hassle.

🔄

Auto-Updates

The manifest stays fresh. New content lands in your store automatically.

How It Works

From zero to playing in minutes

01

Add the Manifest

→
02

Launch the Store

Open Jail X360 from your scripts menu on the console.

→
03

Browse & Download

→
04

Play

No PC required. Just pure homebrew on your 360.
