About

Built For The Community

Digital Den 360 brings the scattered Xbox 360 homebrew scene into one place. Games, emulators, utilities, and themes — curated, tested, and delivered straight to your console.

Learn More →
How It Works

From Zero To Playing

01

Add the Manifest

Drop the Digital Den 360 manifest URL into your Aurora dashboard.

02

Launch the Den

Open Digital Den 360 from your scripts menu on the console.

03

Browse & Download

Pick your game, emulator, or tool — it installs straight to your console.

04

Play

No PC required. Just pure homebrew on your 360.
