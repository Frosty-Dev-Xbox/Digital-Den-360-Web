About Digital Den 360

Digital Den 360 is the homebrew store for jailbroken Xbox 360 consoles. Browse and download games, emulators, utilities, and themes directly to your console — no PC required.

Digital Den 360 is a community-driven homebrew store for jailbroken Xbox 360 consoles (RGH, JTAG, and BadUpdate). The store integrates with Aurora 0.7b and lets users browse a curated catalog of homebrew games, emulators, utilities, and themes — all installable directly from the console. Founded by Frosty-Dev-Xbox in 2026, Digital Den 360 aims to preserve and celebrate the Xbox 360 homebrew scene.

Digital Den 360 only hosts homebrew and open-source content. No retail games, no XBLA titles, no piracy — ever.
