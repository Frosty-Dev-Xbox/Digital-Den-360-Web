Install

Full installation guide. Unlocks when the beta drops.

01

Prepare Your Console

Make sure your Xbox 360 is jailbroken (RGH, JTAG, or BadUpdate) and running Aurora 0.7b or later.

02

Download the Script

Once beta opens, download jailx360.lua from GitHub Releases.

03

Copy to Your Console

Transfer the script to Hdd:/Aurora/User/Scripts/Utility/ via FTP or USB.

04

Launch From Aurora

Open Aurora, go to Scripts, and select Jail X360. The store opens.

05

Browse & Install

Pick your game, emulator, or tool. Hit download. It installs straight to your console.
