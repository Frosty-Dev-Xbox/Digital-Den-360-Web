Guide

How To Install

01

Prepare Your Console

Jailbroken 360 (RGH, JTAG, or BadUpdate) running Aurora 0.7b or later.

02

Download the Script

Once beta opens, download digitalden.lua from GitHub Releases.

03

Copy to Console

Transfer to Hdd:/Aurora/User/Scripts/Utility/ via FTP or USB.

04

Launch from Aurora

Open Aurora, go to Scripts, select Digital Den 360. The den opens.

05

Sign In (Premium)

Premium and Founder members sign in with GitHub to unlock exclusive content.

06

Browse & Install

Pick your game, emulator, or tool. Hit download. It installs straight to your console.
