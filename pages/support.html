Common issues

Check your console's internet connection. Digital Den 360 needs to reach GitHub to fetch the manifest. If your 360 is offline, the store can't sync.

Try again — GitHub Releases occasionally rate-limit. If it keeps failing, Premium members get priority download servers that bypass most rate limits.

Check the compatibility page for your dashboard. Some homebrew requires DashLaunch plugins. Aurora 0.7b is recommended for best results.

Badges sync with your GitHub account. Make sure your GitHub username is linked in your Digital Den 360 settings. If it still doesn't show, open an issue.
