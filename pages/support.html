Troubleshooting

Check your console's internet connection. Digital Den 360 needs to reach GitHub to fetch the manifest. If your 360 is offline, the den can't sync.

Try again — GitHub Releases occasionally rate-limit. Premium members get priority servers that bypass most rate limits.

Check the compatibility page for your dashboard. Some homebrew requires DashLaunch plugins. Aurora 0.7b is recommended.

Badges sync with your GitHub account. Make sure your GitHub username is linked in your settings. If it still doesn't show, open an issue.

Yes. Premium and Founder tiers are tied to your account, not your console. Sign in on the new console with the same GitHub account.

All tiers are non-refundable once access is granted. If you have an issue, contact us and we'll sort it out.
