About

The story behind Jail X360.

🔓 What is Jail X360?

Jail X360 is a homebrew store for jailbroken Xbox 360 consoles. It brings together games, emulators, utilities, and themes into one place — downloadable straight to your console without needing a PC.

🎯 Why build it?

The Xbox 360 homebrew scene is alive and thriving, but finding quality content is scattered across forums and old file hosts. Jail X360 fixes that — a single, curated, always-updated store.

🧑‍💻 Who made it?

Jail X360 is built by Frosty-Dev-Xbox. It's a passion project — not a company, not a business. Just someone who loves the homebrew community.

📜 Legal & ethical

Jail X360 only hosts homebrew and open-source content. No retail games, no XBLA, no piracy. Ever. This is about supporting creators, not stealing from them.
