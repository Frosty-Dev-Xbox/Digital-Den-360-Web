Behind The Den

The story behind Digital Den 360.

💗

What Is It?

Digital Den 360 is a homebrew store for jailbroken Xbox 360 consoles. Games, emulators, utilities, and themes — all in one place.

🎯

Why Build It?

The 360 homebrew scene is scattered across forums and dead file hosts. Digital Den 360 fixes that — one curated, always-updated den.

✦

Why Premium?

Building and maintaining a store takes time and money. Premium and Founder help cover servers and support homebrew devs.

🧑‍💻

Who Made It?

Built by Frosty-Dev-Xbox. A passion project, not a business.

📜

Legal & Ethical

Homebrew and open-source only. No retail games, no XBLA, no piracy. Ever.

🤝

Community

Open source, community-driven. Submit your own homebrew and get featured.

The Mission

Preserving The 360 Scene

Digital Den 360 exists to celebrate and preserve the incredible Xbox 360 homebrew community. Every game, emulator, and tool featured here is made by passionate developers who deserve recognition.
